# Synthetic telemetry examples

`normal.csv` and `voltage_degradation.csv` derive from the recovered demonstration files in `samples/`. Latitude/longitude columns have been removed. Vehicle ID `DEMO01` is synthetic. These are not real R15, SAN-V1, customer or VED research records.

Signals retain the original explicit units in their headers. Missing signals must not be inferred as measured. They exercise a software workflow; they do not establish diagnostic accuracy. SHA-256 values are recorded in the dated verification report.
